# IDEAS FOR TOMORROW

## Mode 2 Drawing Enhancements
- [ ] Real-time Time Estimation: Show estimated move time *while* drawing.
- [ ] Time Efficiency Gradient: Update the cursor/text color based on path length:
    - WHITE: Very short time.
    - GREEN: Optimal (~10 seconds).
    - BLUE: Medium (~20 seconds).
    - RED: Long (~30 seconds).
    - DIM WHITE: Max (~40 seconds).
- [ ] Real-time Stats: Show current Step count, Distance, and Time on the canvas *during* the drawing phase.

## Mode 2 Hardware Integration
- [ ] 7-Segment Display Commands: Show current movement command on the 7-seg LEDs:
    - 'L' for Left.
    - 'F' for Forward.
    - 'S' for Stop.
    - 'A' for Right (since 'R' is not possible on 7-seg).

## Mode 3 Concept Ideas
1. **AI Patrol Mode:** Car moves in a continuous square/circle loop. LCD shows a "Radar" sweep. Laser fires automatically at simulated targets.
2. **Live Record & Playback:** Drive the car live (Mode 1 style) while recording joystick inputs. Then, replay the exact live performance automatically.
3. **Rhythmic Dance Mode:** Car performs a pre-programmed sequence of moves synchronized with RGB LED patterns and a BPM counter on the 7-segment display.
4. **Joystick Precision Mission:** Move at very low speeds to reach a target on the LCD. The 7-segment display acts as a "Noise Meter"—don't move too jerky or you fail!
5. **Virtual Laser Turret:** Car remains stationary. Use Joystick/Touch to control a crosshair on the LCD to shoot moving targets on the screen with the physical laser.
